# Report template

```markdown
# Should I contribute to owner/repo?

**Verdict: GO | GO-IF | NO-GO**  
**Confidence: High | Medium | Low**  
**Lens: user's stated goal | general newcomer outlook**  
**Researched:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Bottom line

Answer directly in 2–4 sentences. For GO-IF, name the conditions. For NO-GO, name the direct blocker or supported combination of negatives.

## Evidence

| Dimension | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Access | positive/mixed/negative/unavailable | linked metrics and examples |
| Maintenance | … | … |
| Governance | … | … |
| Fit | … | … |

## Contribution landscape

| Metric | Result | Population/window/source |
|---|---|---|
| Open PRs | … | exact total; census or sampled pages |
| Outside open PRs | … | human PRs; association rule |
| Open >30d / >90d | … | … |
| Outside closed outcomes | merged/total | completed UTC window; bots excluded |
| Newcomer outcomes | merged/total | FIRST_TIMER + FIRST_TIME_CONTRIBUTOR |
| Maintainer response | … | method and sample |

## Risks, opportunities, and contrary evidence

- Evidence-backed risk.
- Evidence-backed opportunity.
- Strongest fact that points against the selected verdict and why it does not change the result.

## Recommended next step

Give one actionable step: discuss a scoped issue, choose a matching labeled task, prepare a small PR, wait for a maintainer response, or invest elsewhere.

## Method and limitations

- Canonical repository and host.
- Collection depth and completed UTC window.
- Bot, core/outside, and unknown association rules.
- Sample sizes, truncated categories, unavailable evidence, and likely effect on confidence.
- Links to source evidence and, when saved, `evidence.json`.
```
